About the text

  • Title and author (from source header): Qaṣídiy-i-Varqá’íyyih (Ode of the Dove) (`قصيده عز ورقائيه`, Qaṣídiy-i-‘Izz-i-Varqá’íyyih), by Bahá’u’lláh.
  • Edition and source: Saved at [source] from `https://oceanoflights.org/bahaullah-st-092-ar/` (retrieved 2026-10-17), which cites Áthár-i-Qalam-i-A‘lá, vol. 2, 159 B.E., pp. 321–339 (`آثار قلم اعلى، جلد ٢، ١٥٩ بديع، الصفحات ٣٢١ -٣٣٩`).
  • Language and form: Classical Arabic mono-rhyme ode (qaṣídih) of 127 verses (254 hemistichs) rhyming in -atí / -ati (tá’íyyih), preceded by a single-line Arabic prose preface (`في مدح المحبوب سرّا دون الجهر قصيدة عزّ ورقائيّة في جوهر روح قدسيّة`). The source text contains no stanza breaks. Frequently in this ode, nouns and verbs at the rhyme position adopt a feminine ending (-at / -ah) or adjusted morphology to sustain the -atí / -ati rhyme.
  • What the poem itself says:
  • Preface: Declares the work to be ”In praise of the Beloved secretly, not openly: an Ode of Dove-like Glory concerning the Essence of a Most Holy Spirit.“
  • Verses 1–36 (The Lover’s praise and lamentation): The speaker describes being drawn in rapture by the lightning-flashes of the Beloved’s Countenance—before Whose manifestation all suns are eclipsed, the musk of the Unseen Cloud (al-‘amá’) is stirred, the Trumpet of Resurrection is blown, the Sinai of Eternity is revealed, and the soul of Moses (al-Kalím) is purified (vv. 1–10). The speaker then pours out His grief in separation (furqah / hajr) and yearning for reunion (vaṣl), spreading His heart beneath Her foot (v. 12), receiving the stroke of the sword when raising His hands for union (v. 15), bearing the sorrow of Ḥusayn (v. 20), weeping tears beside which the encompassing ocean is a drop, and burning with an anguish beside which Abraham’s fire (náru’l-Khalíl) is a single ember (v. 26), while the Maidens of the chambers (ḥúru’l-quṣúr) array themselves in black robes in every lofty chamber out of the grief of His inmost heart (v. 35).
  • Verses 37–61 (The Beloved’s first address): The Beloved calls out from behind Him, bidding Him be silent and restrain His tongue (`أن اصمت فخذ لسانك`, v. 37). She declares that many a Ḥusayn, many an ‘Alí, and many a lover and chosen one like Him have longed for Her in every age without attaining the light of reunion (vv. 38–40). She proclaims Her transcendent sovereignty: before Her dawning-place the Sun of Manifestation is as a star (v. 41), at Her glance the Moses of Eternity swooned away and Sinai was crushed to dust (v. 46), at the breathing of Her spirit the mouldering bones quivered with life (v. 47), the Reality of the Cause circumambulated Her House (v. 48), and the letter Bá’ of open declaration fell prostrate in secret before Her Point (v. 49). She rebukes the speaker for approaching Her with attributes, names, and selfhood—calling limitation ”the greatest sin“ (vv. 52–54)—and ordains the law of love: drinking the affliction of time from every cup, shedding blood, burning the vitals, sleepless nights, daily reviling, and taking deadly poison as a draught and the wrath of destiny as compassion (vv. 56–61).
  • Verses 62–97 (The Lover’s secret reply and self-offering): The speaker calls out to Her secretly (`وناديتها سرّا`), presenting Himself before the hands of Her power and offering His breast to the spears of Her might and His body to the swords of wrath (vv. 62–66). He recounts being assailed daily with spears of rejection and accusations of infidelity (kufr) and idolatry (shirk) (vv. 68–71), and declares that the archetypal sorrows of Jacob, Joseph, Job, Abraham, Adam, Jonah, David, Noah, Eve, Mary, Isaiah, and Zachariah were all decreed from a mere trickle (rashḥ) of His grief and manifested from the overflow (ṭafḥ) of His care (vv. 72–75). He describes wandering through the lands without a companion (v. 76), the mark of the iron collar upon His neck and the trace of the fetters upon His feet (v. 81), burning every day through the allusion of verse or the plain utterance of prose (v. 82), ascending in His secret heart to the fount of meeting (v. 87), attaining the Exalted Light on the day of His mission (`يوم بعثتي`, v. 91), departing in exile from Ṭá’ (`وهاجرت بالطّاء في عهد غربتي`, v. 92), and finally bidding His spirit, His heart, and His patience endure all hardship and ease in the Beloved’s good-pleasure (vv. 94–97).
  • Verses 98–127 (The Beloved’s second address and closing beatitudes): Speaking through the Spirit, the Beloved bids Him be patient and cast aside all that He hath known, declaring that before Her transcendence polytheism and oneness are alike, the glory of Sinai is as chaff, and the brightest light is as darkness (vv. 98–100); His verses of praise are true, yet only for novices (fityah) and subjects (ra‘íyyah) (v. 101). She proclaims that the Psalms of heaven and Scrolls of splendour were sent down from Her Scroll (v. 106), that all divinities were deified from a trickle of Her command and all lords nurtured from the overflow of Her rule (v. 110), and that from the letter Káf of Her command (Kun, ”Be!“) every decree was ordained (v. 115). She counsels Him to hold fast to the Cord of the Cause in outward form while recognizing the Face of Light in the inward unseen (v. 118), to rend the veil of nearness without allusion and behold the Beauty of Holiness within Himself (`فاشهد جمال القدس فيك بلا كشفة`, v. 119), and to guard the Mystery of Holiness veiled within Him beyond the reach of every luminous mind, lest all existence perish in an instant (vv. 120–123). The ode closes with five beatitudes (`فطوبى...`) upon those who attain through faithfulness, those who enter the wondrous Law, the lovers who shed their blood, those who trust in the cord of Her loving-kindness, and the sincere who hasten from all directions beneath the shadow of Her Lordship (vv. 125–127).

Preface

في مدح المحبوب سرّا دون الجهر قصيدة عزّ ورقائيّة في جوهر روح قدسيّة

In praise of the Beloved secretly, apart from open declaration: an ode of dove-like glory concerning the essence of a sacred Spirit.

1

أجذبتني بوارق أنوار طلعته

لظهورها كلّ الشّموس تخفّت

The lightning-flashes of the lights of His Countenance drew me in rapture;

at Her manifestation all the suns hid themselves.

2

كأنّ بروق الشّمس من نور حسنها

ظهرت في العالمين وغرّت

It is as though the lightnings of the sun, from the light of Her beauty,

appeared throughout the worlds and dazzled.

3

لبهجتها مسك العماء تهيّجت

لرفعتها روح العلاء تعلّت

At Her joyous beauty, the musk of the Unseen Cloud was stirred into fragrance;

at Her loftiness, the Spirit of the Realm on High was exalted.

4

بنفختها صور القيام تنفّخت

بنفحتها ظلّ الغمام تمرّت

By Her blast, the Trumpet of Resurrection was blown;

by Her fragrant breeze, the shadow of the clouds glided past.

5

بلمعتها طور البقاء تظهّرت

لغرّتها نور البهاء تجلّت

By Her flash, the Sinai of Eternity was made manifest;

at Her radiant brow, the Light of Glory shone forth in effulgence.

6

عن ‌مغربها ‌شمس ‌الظّهور ‌تظهّرت

عن مشرقها بدر الشّهور تكرّت

From Her setting-place, the Sun of Manifestation was made manifest;

from Her dawning-place, the Full Moon of the months revolved in its cycles.

7

و‌عن ‌شعرها ‌طيب الشّمال تنفّحت

وعن‌طرفها ‌عين الجمال‌ تقرّت

And from Her hair, the sweet perfume of the north wind wafted forth;

and through Her glance, the eye of Beauty was solaced.

8

بنور وجهها وجه الهدى قد اهتدى

بنار طلعتها نفس الكليم تزكت

By the light of Her Face, the face of guidance hath been guided aright;

by the fire of Her Countenance, the soul of Him Who conversed with God [Moses] was purified.

9

لسهم شفره صدر ‌الصّدور‌ تقبّلت

لوهق جعدها رأس الوجود تمدّت

To the arrow of Her [lit. His] eyelash, the breast of breasts stepped forward in welcome;

to the lasso of Her curling lock, the head of existence stretched itself forth.

10

وغايتي القصوى مواقع رجلها

وعرش العماء أرض عليها تمشّت

And my furthest goal is the places where Her foot steppeth,

and the Throne of the Unseen Cloud is ground whereupon She walked.

11

وفي كلّ عين قد بكيت لوصلها

وفي كلّ نار قد حرقت لفرقتي

And with every eye have I wept for reunion with Her,

and in every fire have I burned because of my separation.

12

بسطت بكلّ البسط لالقاء رجلها

على‌ قلبي ‌وهذا من‌ أوّل منيتي

I spread myself out with all openness for the placing of Her foot

upon my heart, and this is from the first of my wish.

13

طلبت حضور الوصل في كلّ وجهة

رقمت‌ حروف ‌القرب ‌فوق ‌كلّ ‌تربة

I sought the presence of reunion in every direction;

I inscribed the letters of nearness upon every tract of dust.

14

ولو كنت سارعا في وصل نورها

رميت برمي البعد من بعد ‌قربتي

And even though I was hastening toward reunion with Her light,

I was cast away with the casting of remoteness after my nearness.

15

وإن رفعت أيداي في مدّ وصلها

بالسّيف ‌جابتني ‌فذاك‌ جزاء أحبّتي

And when I raised my hands in reaching out for reunion with Her,

with the sword She answered me—and such is the recompense of my loved ones.

16

وهمّي لم يك إلّا لوثق عروة

وقصده ‌لم يك إلّا لقظع‌نسبتي

And my care was naught save for the fastening of a firm cord,

whilst Her [lit. His] purpose was naught save for the severing of my connection.

17

قلت‌ لها ‌روحي ‌فداك ‌و‌ما بي ‌لقاك

أرحمي ‌فلا ‌تكشف ‌عنّي‌ ‌فضيحتي

I said unto Her: ”May my spirit be Thy ransom, and what aileth me is meeting Thee;

have mercy, and lay not bare from me my disgrace!

18

ومنّي بفرط الحبّ عنك بوصلة

إبقائه باقيا في زمان القديمة

And graciously bestow upon me, out of the excess of love, a union from Thee,

whose continuance may endure in ancient time!“

19

وسرّ ظهور لاح من ظهورها

كلّ ‌الورى و‌بالأصل‌ قامت ‌قيامتي

And a mystery of manifestation shone forth from Her manifestation

unto all mankind, and at the very root my resurrection was raised up.

20

وحزن حسين قد احملت لحزنها

كور‌ الوجود في كون قدوتي

And the grief of Ḥusayn have I borne on account of grief for Her,

in the cycle of existence, in the being of my exemplar.

21

لأنت رجا قلبي ومحبوب سرّتي

و‌مالك روحي و‌نوري و‌‌مهجتي

Truly Thou art the hope of my heart and the Beloved of mine inmost heart,

and the Possessor of my spirit, and my light, and my lifeblood!

22

و‌منّي ‌بفوز ‌الوصل من بعد هجرة

و‌هبني ‌بروح ‌الانس‌من بعد ‌كربتي

And graciously confer upon me the attainment of reunion after separation,

and grant me the spirit of fellowship after mine anguish!

23

و‌من‌ حرقتي‌ نار ‌الوقود ‌توقّدت

و‌من‌ زفرتي ‌نور ‌الشّهود ‌تذوّت

And from my burning, the fuel-fed fire was kindled;

and from my sighing, the light of witnessing withered away.

24

بحر العماء من حرّ ظمأي يابس

ونهر‌ السّنا لن‌ يسقني ‌بعض ‌عطشتي

The Ocean of the Unseen Cloud, from the heat of my thirst, is dry,

and the river of splendour shall never slake even a part of my thirst.

25

بكلّ تراب كلّ ثأر شهدته

ها إنّها عن دم عيني تحكت

In every dust, every shed blood that I have witnessed—

behold, verily it telleth the tale of the blood of mine eye.

26

و‌عن دمعتي ‌بحر ‌المحيط ‌كقطرة

و‌من حرقتي نار الخليل كجذوة

And beside my tear, the encompassing ocean is as a drop;

and beside my burning, the fire of the Friend [Abraham] is as a single ember.

27

و‌من‌ حزني بحر السّرور تجمّدت

وعن همّي عين الهموم تجرّت

And from my grief, the sea of joy froze solid;

and from my care, the wellspring of cares flowed forth.

28

سنائي اغمى ضيائي اغشى

ونوري ‌اطفى‌ من ‌غر مشمتي

My splendour swooned away, my radiance was darkened,

and my light was extinguished from the sharpness of the gloating of my foes.

29

عظامي أبرى ‌و‌جسمي أبلى

وقلبي أحرى من حرّ حرقتي

My bones are whittled away and my body is worn out,

and my heart is consumed from the heat of my burning.

30

هواك هباني وحبّك حكّني

و‌هجرك‌ ذابني ‌و‌وصلك‌ منيّتي

Passion for Thee hath scattered me as dust-motes, and love for Thee hath worn me away,

and separation from Thee hath melted me, and reunion with Thee is my wish [or: my death].

31

وعن‌ سرّ‌ حزني‌ كاد ‌السّماء ‌تفطّرت

ومن ‌همّ ‌قلبي‌ أرض‌الفؤاد تشقّت

And from the mystery of my grief, the heaven was well-nigh rent asunder;

and from the care of my heart, the earth of the inner heart was cleft open.

32

وعن حرّ قلبي دمع عيني حاكيا

ومن زفر سرّي ‌صفر‌ وجهي‌تدلّت

And of the heat of my heart, the tear of mine eye is telling;

and to the sighing of mine inmost heart, the paleness of my face bore witness.

33

أحنّ بكلّ ‌اللّيل‌من ‌شمت معذلي

الحّ ‌بكلّ‌ يوم ‌من فقد‌ ‌نصرتي

I moan in yearning throughout every night from the gloating of my censurer;

I cry out throughout every day from the loss of my succour.

34

وصلت إلى غاية الذّلّ رتبة

عن ذكرها ‌كلّ اللّسان تكلّت

I have reached, unto the uttermost limit of abasement, a station

from whose mention every tongue hath grown weary.

35

حور ‌القصور ‌من‌ حزن ‌سرّي ‌تقمّصت

قميص السّود في كلّ غرفة

The Maidens of the chambers, out of the grief of mine inmost heart, have clothed themselves

in the robe of black in every lofty chamber.

36

وردت بكلّ الحزن في كلّ قلبة

قبضت‌ بكلّ ‌القبض‌ في ‌كلّ بسطة

I entered with all grief into every turning of the heart;

I was contracted with all contraction in every expansion.

37

ونادتني ‌من ‌ورائي ‌و‌قالت‌ أن‌ اصمت

فخذ ‌لسالك‌ عن ‌كلّ ‌ما قد‌ تحكّت‌

And She called out unto me from behind me and said: ”Be silent!

And withhold thy tongue from all that it hath recounted!

38

فكم من حسين بمثلك قد أرادني

فكم من عليّ كشبهك ‌من ‌أحبّتي

For how many a Ḥusayn like unto thee hath desired Me!

How many an ‘Alí resembling thee hath been among My loved ones!

39

فكم من حبيب فوقك قد أحبّني

فكم‌ من ‌صفيّ ‌كفوك من ‌أهل ‌صفوتي

How many a lover above thee hath loved Me!

How many a chosen one, thy peer, hath been among the people of My choice!

40

فقد ‌ضجّ في‌كلّ الآوان و‌لن يفز

بنور ‌‌الوصل لحضا إليّ بنظرتي

Yet he cried out in lamentation at all times and shall never attain

unto the light of reunion, glancing toward Me for a single look from Me!

41

ومن ‌مشرقي ‌شمس‌ الظّهور ‌كنجمة

وعن مظهري نور البسيط كلمعة

And before My dawning-place, the Sun of Manifestation is as a single star;

and beside My place of manifestation, the all-encompassing light is as a single flash.

42

ومن نور سرّي سرّ الوجود كنملة

ومن ‌نار حبّي ‌نار ‌الوقود ‌كقبسة

And beside the light of Mine inmost mystery, the mystery of existence is as an ant;

and beside the fire of My love, the fuel-fed fire is as a single spark.

43

وعن فطرتي فطر الاله تديّنت

وعن ‌كفّتي كف‌ السّناء تضمّت

And through My primordial nature, the divine natures embraced religion;

and from My palm, the hand of splendour was gathered together.

44

وقد جاء أمر الأمر من أمر ظاهر

وقد جاء عدل الحكم من عدل حكمتي

And the Command of the Cause hath come from a manifest command,

and the justice of rule hath come from the justice of My wisdom.

45

وموج البحر قد كفّ من موج باطني

وروح القدس قد هاج من نور بهجتي

And the wave of the sea hath been stayed before the wave of Mine inward being,

and the Holy Spirit hath been stirred into commotion from the light of My joyous beauty.

46

وعن ‌نظرتي موسى ‌البقاء ‌تصعّقت

ومن لمعتي طور الجبال تدكّت

And at My glance, the Moses of Eternity swooned away;

and from My flash, the Sinai of the mountains was crushed to dust.

47

عن نشر أمري روح النّفوس تحشّرت

من نفخ روحي‌ عظم الرّميم ‌تهزّت

Through the unfolding of My Cause, the spirits of all souls were gathered together;

through the breathing of My spirit, the mouldering bones quivered with life.

48

وقد طاف نفس الأمر في ‌حول بيتها

وروح البيت قد قام من نور طلعتي

And the very Reality of the Cause hath circumambulated round Her House,

and the Spirit of the House hath stood firm through the light of My Countenance.

49

و‌ملك معالي العلم في الباء سرّة

وباء الجهر بالسّرّ خرّت لنقطتي

And the kingdom of the heights of knowledge is a secret within the Bá’,

and the Bá’ of open declaration fell down prostrate in secret before My Point.

50

كلّ الهدى من فجر أمري قد بدا

وكلّ‌ العلى قد‌ أوفدت‌ من ‌وفدتي

All guidance hath appeared from the dawn of My Cause,

and all exaltedness hath been sent forth from Mine embassy.

51

وعن نعمتي غنّ الطّيور كلحنة

ومن غنّيتي لحن النّحول كرّنة

And beside My melody [lit. favour], the singing of the birds is as a single note;

and beside My song, the hum of the bees [or: of wasted lovers] is as a single buzz.

52

شرعت بسوء‌ الظّنّ عنك شريعة

شربت‌ بحبّ ‌الغير ‌عن ‌دون ‌شرعتي

Thou hast devised, out of thine evil surmise, a law;

thou hast drunk of the love of others, apart from My watering-place.

53

وجئت بأوصاف اتيت بنسبة

و‌رمت‌ بأسماء ‌عن ‌سواء ‌محجّتي

And thou hast come with descriptions, thou hast brought a lineage,

and thou hast sought through names away from the straightness of My highway.

54

وصفت بنفس ونسبتها بنفسي

ها هو ‌حدّ فال ‌حدّ أعظم خطيئة

Thou hast described with a self and attributed it unto My Self;

behold, that is a limit, and limitation is the greatest sin!

55

رجوت بظنّك وصلي هيهات

لم ‌يكن ‌بذاك ‌جرى ‌شرط ‌ان وفيّت

Thou didst hope, in thy fancy, for reunion with Me—far, far is that from thee!

Not thus hath run the condition, if thou wouldst fulfil it.

56

توفّت فشرب بلاء الدّهر عن كلّ كاسة

و‌سقى دماء ‌القهر ‌عن دم ‌مهجّة

Fulfil it then: the drinking of the affliction of time from every cup,

and the quaffing of the bloods of wrath from the lifeblood of the heart,

57

وقطع الرّجاء عن مسّ كلّ راحة

وقمع القضاء ‌عن طمع كلّ حاجة

and the severing of hope from touching any comfort,

and the subduing of destiny from coveting any need.

58

سفك‌ الدّماء ‌في ‌مذهب ‌‌العشق ‌واجب

و‌حرق ‌الحش في ‌الحبّ ‌من ‌أوّل ‌بيعتي

The shedding of blood in the creed of love is obligatory,

and the burning of the vitals in love is from the very first of My covenant;

59

يقظ اللّيالي‌ من‌ لذع كلّ ‌ملذع

وشتم ‌التّوالي ‌في ‌كلّ يومة

the wakefulness of the nights from the stinging of every biting pain,

and continual reviling throughout every day.

60

وعن ‌سنّتي‌ سمّ‌ ‌الرّدى‌ كشربة

وعن ملّتي قهر القضاء كشفقة

And according to My way, the poison of destruction is as a draught of water;

and according to My creed, the wrath of divine decree is as tender compassion.

61

خلّ دعوى الحبّ أو فارض بما‌ جرى

كذاك جرى الأمر في فرض سنّتي

Abandon the claim of love, or else be content with what hath come to pass;

even thus hath the command run in the ordinance of My way!“

62

وناديتها سرّا بأن يا حبيبتي

وغاية آمالي ومقصود سرّتي

And I called out unto Her secretly, saying: ”O my Beloved,

and the Ultimate Goal of my hopes and the Object of mine inmost heart!

63

فها أنا حاضر بين يديّ قدرتك

فها أنا آمل بما قد تعدت

Behold, here am I present before the hands of Thy power;

behold, here am I hoping for that which Thou hast promised!

64

فها أنا طالب بكلّ ما أنت تحبّ

فها ‌أنا ‌راكن ‌بما قد تقضّت

Behold, here am I seeking all that Thou lovest;

behold, here am I relying upon that which hath been decreed!

65

صدري هذا راجي لأرماح سطوتك

وجسمي هذا شايق لأسياف قهرة

This breast of mine is hopeful for the spears of Thy might,

and this body of mine is yearning for the swords of wrath.

66

نارك نوري وقهرك بغيتي

وبطشك‌ راحتي ‌وحكمك‌ منيتي

Thy fire is my light, and Thy wrath is my desire,

and Thy fierce assault is my comfort, and Thy judgment is my wish.

67

فانظر إلى دمع عيني كيف تجرّيت

فاشهد بسرّ قلبي كيف اضمحلت

Look Thou, then, upon the tear of mine eye, how it hath flowed;

and bear witness unto the mystery of my heart, how it hath vanished away!

68

رميت رماح الكلّ في كلّ يومة

قتلت بسيف الرّد في كلّ ليلة

I have been shot at with the spears of all men on every day;

I have been slain by the sword of rejection on every night.

69

قرأت كتاب الكفر في كلّ سطرة

وفزت بسبّ الكلّ في كلّ لحظة

I have read the indictment [lit. book] of infidelity in every line,

and I have won the cursing of all at every moment.

70

طعنت بطعن الشّرك في كلّ آنة

رمحت برمح الطّرد في كلّ وقتة

I have been pierced with the taunt of idolatry at every instant;

I have been speared with the spear of banishment at every season.

71

كأنّ بلاء الدّهر لنفسي قد نزل

كأنّ ‌سيوف‌ القهر ‌حدّت لجيدتي

It is as though the affliction of all time had descended upon my soul;

it is as though the swords of wrath had been sharpened for my neck.

72

حزنة يعقوب و‌سجنة ‌يوسف

وضرّة أيّوب ونار خليلة

The grief of Jacob and the prison of Joseph,

and the affliction of Job and the fire of the Friend [Abraham],

73

تأسف آدم وهجرة يونس

وضجّة داود و نوحة نوحة

the sorrow of Adam and the flight of Jonah,

and the lamentation of David and the wailing of Noah,

74

وفرقة حوّاء وحرقة مريم

ومحنة شعياء وكرب زكرّية

and the separation of Eve and the burning anguish of Mary,

and the trial of Isaiah and the distress of Zachariah—

75

من‌ رشح‌ حزني ‌قد ‌قضى ‌لكلّ ‌ما ‌قضى

وعن طفح همّي قد بدا كلّ بليّة

from a trickle of my grief was decreed for each what was decreed,

and from the overflow of my care hath every tribulation appeared!

76

فانظر بسيري في البلاد بلا مونس

فاشهد ‌بآنسي في ‌العراء‌ ‌بوحشة

Look Thou, then, upon my journeying through the lands without a companion,

and bear witness unto my fellowship in the open wilderness with desolation!

77

و‌عن فتح عيني عين السّماء تهمرّت

و‌من فجر‌ ‌قلبي فجر الأرض تلقت

And from the opening of mine eye, the fountain of heaven poured down in torrents;

and from the cleaving of my heart, the gushing springs of the earth met together.

78

ومن روح حزني روح البقاء تقطّعت

و‌عن‌ نور ‌همّي ‌عرش ‌العلاء ‌تهدّت

And from the wind of my grief, the Spirit of Eternity was rent in pieces;

and from the light [or: fire] of my care, the Throne of the Realm on High was shaken down.

79

حمر الوجود من دم قلبي تحمّرت

غصن ‌الشّهود ‌عن ‌دمع ‌عيني ‌تنبّت

The redness of existence was dyed crimson from the blood of my heart;

the Branch of Witnessing sprouted forth from the tear of mine eye.

80

مرّ البلا في سبيل حبّك حلوة

و‌شهد ‌البقاء ‌من ‌عند ‌غيرك مرّة

The bitterness of affliction in the path of Thy love is sweet,

and the honey of immortality from any save Thee is bitter.

81

و‌عن‌ عنق ‌رسم ‌الحديد تعيّنت

و‌من رجلي أثر الوثيق تبقّت

And upon my neck, the mark of the iron hath become visible;

and upon my foot, the trace of the fetter hath remained.

82

ما ‌مضى يوما إلّا وقد ‌حرقت فيه

من تلويح نظم أو تصريح نثرة

Not a day hath passed but that I have burned therein,

whether from the allusion of verse or the plain utterance of prose.

83

روحي قد راح وقلبي قد ذاب

وسرّي قد فار من شدّ شدّتي

My spirit hath departed, and my heart hath melted away,

and mine inmost heart hath boiled over from the fierceness of my distress.

84

بقيت بلا روح وقلب ومهجّة

و‌ابقاء‌ نفسىي كان‌ من ‌أعظم حيرتي

I have remained without spirit, or heart, or lifeblood,

and the survival of my soul hath been among the greatest causes of my bewilderment.

85

من ‌علوّ سرّي ‌قد قضى‌عليّ ما ‌جرى

فيا‌ليت بالأصل ما علت فطرتي

Because of the loftiness of mine inmost heart, there hath been decreed against me what hath come to pass;

would that, at the very root, my primordial nature had never been exalted!

86

كذاك ‌أحاطتني ‌البلا عن ‌كلّ ‌شطرة

بذاك ‌أبادتني القض في‌كلّ‌حينة

Even thus hath affliction encompassed me on every side;

thereby hath the divine decree destroyed me at every moment.

87

عرجت إلى غاية الوحد وحدة

وصلت‌ إلى‌ عين ‌اللّقاء في ‌سريرتي

I ascended unto the pinnacle of oneness, alone;

I attained unto the very fount of meeting within my secret heart.

88

وصفك في وصف عيني شهدته

عن ‌عينك‌ في‌كلّ ‌طرف‌ حديدة

Thy description, in the description of mine own essence [or: eye], have I witnessed,

from Thine own Essence [or: Eye], in every piercing glance.

89

إن كنت بالحدّ فالحدّ منك ظاهر

و‌لو‌ بالوصف‌ فالوصف ‌منك‌ تبدت

If I be with limitation, then the limit from Thee is manifest;

and even if with description, then the description from Thee hath appeared.

90

وعن كدرتي ظلم اللّيال تحقّقت

وعن سرّتي نور النّهار تصفّت

And from my gloom, the darknesses of the nights were realized;

and from mine inmost heart [or: my joy], the light of day shone pure.

91

فلا بأس إن صرت مطرودا لأن

فزت بالنّور العلي يوم بعثتي

Yet it mattereth not if I have become an outcast, for

I attained unto the Exalted Light on the day of my mission.

92

وآنست بالقدس من نور آنسه

وهاجرت بالطّاء في عهد غربتي

And I found fellowship with holiness from the light of His intimacy [or: the light He perceived],

and I departed in exile from Ṭá’ [Ṭihrán] in the time of my banishment.

93

وآمنت بالنّور من نور باطني

وعارجت بالرّوح في سرّ سرّتي

And I believed in the Light from the light of mine inward being,

and I ascended with the Spirit in the mystery of mine inmost heart.

94

أناديك يا روح الحيوة أن ارتحل

من ‌نفس ‌ما ‌بقى فيه من بقيّة

I call unto thee, O Spirit of Life: Depart thou

from a soul wherein no remnant hath remained!

95

فيا روح العماء ‌من العرش أنزلي

فما لك قدر بمقدار ذلّتي

And O Spirit of the Unseen Cloud, descend thou from the Throne,

for thou hast no measure equal to the measure of mine abasement!

96

أصاحي يا فؤادي أن أخرجي

فما لك من عزّ في بلاد ذليلة

O my companion, O mine inner heart, come thou forth,

for there is no glory for thee in a land of abasement!

97

فيا ‌صبري ‌اصبر ‌في ‌كلّ ما ‌شهدته

في رضا ‌حبيبك‌ من ‌شد ورخوة

And O my patience, be thou patient in all that thou hast witnessed,

in the good-pleasure of thy Beloved, whether of hardship or of ease!“

98

بالرّوح نادتني وقالت أن اصبر

فقد ‌عرفت بكلّ ما ‌أنت استدّلت

Through the Spirit She called out unto me and said: ”Be patient,

for thou hast known through all whereby thou hast sought proof.

99

دع عنك ما عرفت وبه قد

عكفت فالشّرك عندي كوحدة

Cast aside from thyself what thou hast known and whereunto

thou hast cleaved, for polytheism in My sight is even as oneness!

100

أبهى بهاء الطّور عندي كحشوة

و‌أسنى ضياء ‌النّور ‌عندي ‌كظلمة

The most glorious glory of Sinai in My sight is as mere stuffing [or: chaff],

and the most resplendent radiance of light in My sight is as darkness.

101

آيات وصفك حقّ و‌لكن لفتية

آثا‌ر نعتك‌ صدق ولكن لرعيّتي

The verses of thy description are true, yet only for youths [or: novices];

the traces of thy praise are truthful, yet only for My subjects.

102

وإنّي ‌لم يزل قد كنت في ‌قدسة

و‌إنّى ‌لن‌ يحد قد ‌كنت في نزهة

For verily I, everlastingly, have abided in holiness;

and verily I, beyond all limit, have abided in transcendent sanctity.

103

فكم ‌من ‌عادل ‌قد كان ‌عندي ‌ظالما

فكم‌ من‌ عالم ‌قد كان ‌عندي ‌كجهلة

How many a just one hath been in My sight a wrongdoer!

How many a learned one hath been in My sight as the ignorant!

104

فكم ‌من ‌باقي ‌قد ‌كان ‌عندي ‌فانيا

فكم ‌من ‌عارف ‌لن يعرف بحرفة

How many an abiding one hath been in My sight evanescent!

How many a mystic knower shall never know even a single letter!

105

فكم ‌من ‌عابد ‌قد كان ‌عندي ‌طاغيا

فكم‌ من‌ ساجد لن‌ يفرّ وقتا بسجدتي

How many a worshipper hath been in My sight a rebel!

How many a prostrating one shall never attain [lit. flee] for a moment unto prostration before Me!

106

زبر ‌السّماء في ‌كون نفسي ثابت

صحف السّنا ‌قد أنزلت ‌من ‌صحيفتي

The Psalms of heaven are established in the being of My Self;

the Scrolls of splendour have been sent down from My Scroll.

107

ومن ذرّتي شمس المحيط تكوّرت

و‌عن‌ قطرتي بحر ‌الوجود ‌تسبّحت

And from Mine atom, the sun of the encompassing sphere was wound round;

and from My drop, the ocean of existence swam forth [or: uttered praise].

108

كلّ الغنا من أهل الورى ظهر

عندي كغنّة نمل أو كرنّة نحلة

All the singing [or: riches] that hath appeared from the people of the world

is in My sight as the hum of an ant or as the buzz of a bee.

109

كلّ ‌العقول ‌من ‌جذب ‌سرّي ‌تولّهت

كلّ ‌النّفوس ‌عن ‌غنّ ‌روحي ‌تحيّت

All minds, through the attraction of Mine inmost mystery, were enraptured;

all souls, through the melody of My spirit, were quickened to life.

110

كلّ ‌الالوه من‌ رشح أمري تألّهت

و‌كلّ ‌الرّبوب عن ‌طفح‌ حكمي ‌تربّت

All divinities, from the trickle of My command, were endowed with divinity;

and all lords, from the overflow of My rule, were nurtured in lordship.

111

أرض الرّوح بالأمر بي قد مشى

و‌عرش‌الطّور قد ‌كان موضع وظأتي

The earth of the Spirit, by the Command, hath walked through Me,

and the Throne of Sinai hath been the place of My footstep.

112

لنوري نجم الظّهور تجلّيت

لروحي شمس السّرور تجلّت

For My light, the Star of Manifestation shone forth;

for My spirit, the Sun of Joy was revealed in effulgence.

113

جوامع آيات لوامع نزلة

مواقع آثار مطالع قدسة

Repositories of verses, gleaming flashes of revelation,

places where traces fall, dawning-places of holiness,

114

جواهر أفكار سواذج فكرة

طرائز أنوار برائز حكمة

gems of thoughts, pure essences of reflection,

broidered ornaments of lights, manifest tokens of wisdom—

115

من ‌كاف ‌أمري قد‌ قضى‌ لكلّ ‌حكمها

وعن لطف سرّي قد بدا كلّ بديعة

from the Káf of My Command, the decree of each hath been ordained,

and from the subtlety of Mine inmost mystery, every wondrous thing hath appeared!

116

أعرضت عن وجهي وبظنّك اقبلتها

و‌أجريت ماء الزّعم في ‌شريعة وهمة

Thou didst turn away from My Face, and by thine own surmise didst approach them,

and thou didst cause the water of conjecture to flow in the watercourse of idle fancy.

117

ما استقمت بنور ‌الغيب فيما صنعة

في نفسك وكذا ضيّعت صنعتي

Thou didst not stand firm in the light of the Unseen in that which thou didst fashion

within thyself, and thus didst thou forfeit My handiwork.

118

تمسّك بحبل الأمر في ظاهر صورة

تعرّف بوجه النّور في باطن غيبة

Hold fast unto the Cord of the Cause in the outward form;

seek recognition of the Face of Light in the inward unseen.

119

فاخرق حجاب القرب عنك بلا رمزة

فاشهد جمال القدس فيك بلا كشفة

Rend asunder, then, the veil of nearness from thyself without an allusion,

and behold the Beauty of Holiness within thyself without an unveiling!

120

فاسكن فإنّ قواة العرش اضطرب

Preface

In secret praise of the Beloved, withheld from open proclamation: an Ode of the Dove of Glory, concerning the Essence of the Most Holy Spirit.

1

The lightning-flashes of the splendours of His Countenance have drawn Me in rapture; before Her manifestation every sun hath hidden its face.

2

Methinks the very lightnings of the sun have dawned forth from the light of Her beauty and dazzled all the worlds.

3

At Her joyous beauty, the musk of the Unseen Cloud hath been stirred into fragrance; at Her exaltation, the Spirit of the Realm on High hath been uplifted.

4

Through Her breath, the Trumpet of Resurrection hath been sounded; through Her fragrant breeze, the shadow of the clouds hath glided past.

5

Through Her flash, the Sinai of Eternity hath been revealed; at Her radiant brow, the Light of Glory hath shone forth in effulgence.

6

From Her setting-place, the Sun of Manifestation hath dawned; from Her dayspring, the Full Moon of the months hath revolved in its course.

7

From Her locks, the sweet savours of the north wind have wafted abroad; through Her glance, the eye of Beauty hath been solaced.

8

By the light of Her Face, the countenance of guidance hath been guided aright; by the fire of Her Countenance, the soul of Him Who conversed with God hath been purified.

9

To welcome the shaft of Her eyelash, the foremost of breasts hath stepped forth; to the noose of Her curling lock, the head of all existence hath stretched itself out.

10

My furthest goal is the dust whereon Her foot hath trodden, and the Throne of the Unseen Cloud is the ground whereover She hath walked.

11

With every eye have I wept in longing for reunion with Her, and in every fire have I burned in the pain of my separation.

12

With uttermost openness have I laid bare my heart that She might set Her foot thereon—and this, from the beginning, hath been my foremost desire.

13

In every direction have I sought the presence of reunion; upon every tract of dust have I inscribed the letters of nearness.

14

Yet whenever I hastened toward reunion with Her light, I was cast afar by the darts of remoteness after having drawn nigh.

15

And whenever I raised My hands in supplication for reunion with Her, She answered Me with the sword—and such is the recompense of My loved ones.

16

My sole endeavour was to lay hold upon a firm cord, whilst Her sole purpose was to sever my bond.

17

I cried unto Her: ”May My spirit be a ransom for Thee! All that aileth Me is yearning to meet Thee. Have mercy, and expose not My disgrace!

18

Vouchsafe unto Me, out of the abundance of Thy love, a union from Thy presence that shall endure forever through eternal ages!“

19

From Her manifestation a mystery of revelation hath shone forth upon all mankind, whereby in very truth My resurrection hath come to pass.

20

For grief of Her have I borne the sorrow of Ḥusayn throughout the cycle of existence, following in the footsteps of Mine exemplar.

21

Truly Thou art the hope of My heart and the Beloved of Mine inmost being, the Possessor of My spirit, My light, and My lifeblood!

22

Graciously confer upon Me the grace of reunion after separation, and grant Me the spirit of intimate fellowship after Mine anguish!

23

From My burning, the fuel-fed fire hath been set ablaze; from My sighing, the light of vision hath faded away.

24

The Ocean of the Unseen Cloud is parched by the heat of My thirst, and the river of splendour can never slake a fraction of My drought.

25

In every land of dust, every shed blood that Mine eyes have witnessed telleth the tale of the tears of blood that flow from Mine eyes.

26

Beside My tears, the encompassing ocean is but a drop; beside My burning heart, the fire of Abraham is but a single ember.

27

Through My grief, the sea of joy hath frozen still; through My care, the fount of sorrows hath gushed forth.

28

My splendour hath swooned away, My radiance is overcast, and My light hath been quenched by the bitter gloating of My foes.

29

My bones are wasted, My body is worn away, and My heart is consumed by the fierce heat of Mine anguish.

30

Passion for Thee hath scattered Me as dust, love for Thee hath worn Me down, separation from Thee hath melted Me away, and reunion with Thee is My sole desire.

31

At the secret of My grief, the heavens are well-nigh rent asunder; from the sorrow of My heart, the earth of the soul is cleft in twain.

32

The tears of Mine eyes recount the burning heat of My heart, and the paleness of My face beareth witness to the sighs of Mine inmost being.

33

All the night long I moan at the malice of My censurer; all the day long I cry out in the loss of My succour.

34

Unto the uttermost depths of abasement have I reached a station which every tongue faileth to describe.

35

Out of the grief of Mine inmost heart, the Maidens of Paradise have arrayed themselves in robes of mourning within every celestial chamber.

36

With utter grief have I entered into every turning of the heart; with utter anguish have I been contracted in every season of openness.

37

Then called She unto Me from behind Me, saying: ”Hold Thy peace, and restrain Thy tongue from all that it hath uttered!

38

How many a Ḥusayn like unto Thee hath desired Me! How many an ‘Alí resembling Thee hath been numbered among My loved ones!

39

How many a lover more ardent than Thee hath loved Me! How many a chosen one, Thy peer, hath been reckoned among My chosen!

40

Each cried out in lamentation through every age, yet never attained the light of reunion nor won a single glance from Mine eyes.

41

Before My dayspring, the Sun of Manifestation is but a star; beside My revelation, the all-encompassing light is but a fleeting flash.

42

Beside the light of Mine inmost mystery, the secret of existence is but an ant; beside the fire of My love, the blazing fire is but a spark.

43

Through My primordial nature, the natures created by God have embraced faith; within My palm, the hand of splendour hath been gathered up.

44

From a manifest command hath come the Command of the Cause, and from the justice of My wisdom hath come the justice of rule.

45

The billows of the sea have been stilled before the surging waves of Mine inward being, and the Holy Spirit hath been stirred into rapture by the light of My blissful beauty.

46

At My glance, the Moses of Eternity hath swooned away; at My flash, the Mount of Sinai hath been crushed to dust.

47

Through the unfolding of My Cause, the spirits of all souls have been resurrected; through the breathing of My spirit, the mouldering bones have quivered with life.

48

The very Reality of the Cause hath circled round Her Sanctuary, and the Spirit of the Sanctuary hath stood firm through the light of My Countenance.

49

The sovereignty of the loftiest heights of knowledge lieth hid as a mystery within the letter Bá’, and the Bá’ of open declaration hath fallen prostrate in secret before My Point.

50

All guidance hath dawned forth from the daybreak of My Cause, and all exaltation hath been sent forth from My court.

51

Beside My melody, the singing of the birds is but a single note; beside My song, the hum of the bees is but a faint murmuring.

52

Out of Thine evil surmise hast Thou devised a law of Thine own, and apart from My fount hast Thou drunk of the love of others.

53

Thou hast come with attributes and brought forth a lineage, and through names hast Thou strayed from the straightness of My highway.

54

Thou hast described with a self and ascribed it unto My Self—behold, this is a limitation, and limitation is the greatest of sins!

55

In Thy fancy didst Thou hope for reunion with Me—far, far is that from Thee! Not thus hath the condition been ordained, wouldst Thou fulfil it.

56

Fulfil it then: it is to drain the affliction of time from every cup, and to quaff the blood of wrath from the lifeblood of the heart;

57

It is to sever all hope of touching any comfort, and to subdue every longing for the fulfilment of any need.

58

In the religion of love, the shedding of blood is an ordinance, and the burning of the vitals in love is the very first requirement of allegiance unto Me;

59

It is sleeplessness by night from the sting of every torment, and continual reviling throughout every day.

60

In My way, deadly poison is as a refreshing draught; in My faith, the wrath of divine decree is as tender compassion.

61

Abandon, then, the claim of love, or be content with what hath come to pass; even thus hath the decree been ordained in the statute of My law!“

62

Then secretly I called out unto Her, saying: ”O My Beloved, Thou Ultimate Goal of My hopes and Object of Mine inmost heart!

63

Behold, here am I standing before the hands of Thy power; behold, here am I hoping for that which Thou hast promised!

64

Behold, here am I seeking whatsoever Thou lovest; behold, here am I content with whatsoever hath been decreed!

65

This breast of Mine longeth for the spears of Thy might, and this body of Mine yearneth for the swords of Thy wrath.

66

Thy fire is My light, Thy wrath is My desire, Thy fierce assault is My comfort, and Thy judgment is My wish.

67

Look, then, upon the tears of Mine eyes, how they have streamed forth; and bear witness unto the mystery of My heart, how it hath faded into naught!

68

Every day have I been assailed by the spears of all men; every night have I been slain by the sword of rejection.

69

In every line have I read the indictment of infidelity; at every moment have I reaped the curses of all mankind.

70

At every instant have I been pierced by the taunt of idolatry; at every season have I been transfixed by the lance of banishment.

71

Methinks the tribulations of all time have descended upon My soul, and the swords of wrath have been whetted for My neck.

72

The grief of Jacob and the prison of Joseph, the affliction of Job and the fire of Abraham the Friend,

73

The sorrow of Adam and the exile of Jonah, the lamentation of David and the wailing of Noah,

74

The separation of Eve and the burning anguish of Mary, the trial of Isaiah and the distress of Zachariah—

75

From a mere trickle of My grief was decreed for each what was decreed, and from the overflow of My sorrow hath every tribulation appeared!

76

Behold, then, My wandering through the lands without a companion, and bear witness unto My fellowship with desolation in the open wilderness!

77

From the opening of Mine eyes, the fountains of heaven have poured down in torrents; from the cleaving of My heart, the gushing springs of the earth have met together.

78

From the tempest of My grief, the Spirit of Eternity hath been rent asunder; from the flame of My sorrow, the Throne of the Realm on High hath been shaken down.

79

All the redness of existence hath been dyed crimson with the blood of My heart, and the Branch of Witnessing hath sprouted forth from the tears of Mine eyes.

80

The bitterness of affliction in the path of Thy love is sweet, and the honey of immortality from any save Thee is bitter.

81

Upon My neck the mark of the iron collar hath been fixed, and upon My feet the trace of the fetters hath remained.

82

Not a day hath passed wherein I have not burned, whether through the allusions of verse or the plain utterances of prose.

83

My spirit hath departed, My heart hath melted away, and Mine inmost being hath boiled over from the intensity of My distress.

84

I have remained without spirit, without heart, without lifeblood, and the very survival of My soul hath been the greatest cause of My bewilderment.

85

Because of the loftiness of Mine inmost heart hath all that transpired been decreed upon Me; would that from the beginning My primordial nature had never been exalted so high!

86

Even thus hath affliction compassed Me round on every side; even thus hath the divine decree consumed Me at every moment.

87

Alone have I ascended unto the pinnacle of oneness; within My secret heart have I attained unto the very fount of meeting.

88

Thy description have I witnessed in the description of Mine own Essence, gazing from Thine own Eye with every piercing glance.

89

If I appear in limitation, that limit is manifest from Thee; and if in description, that description hath shone forth from Thee.

90

From My gloom, the darkness of the nights hath been realized; from Mine inmost heart, the light of day hath shone forth in purity.

91

Yet it mattereth naught if I have become an outcast, for on the day of My mission I attained unto the Exalted Light.

92

I found fellowship with holiness through the light of His intimacy, and in the time of My banishment I departed in exile from the land of Ṭá’.

93

I believed in the Light through the light of Mine inward being, and ascended with the Spirit in the mystery of Mine inmost heart.

94

I call unto thee, O Spirit of Life: Depart from a soul wherein no remnant of life remaineth!

95

O Spirit of the Unseen Cloud, descend from the Throne, for thou hast no worth beside the measure of Mine abasement!

96

O my companion, O Mine inner heart, come forth, for there is no glory for thee in a land of abasement!

97

And O My patience, be thou patient under all that thou hast witnessed of hardship and ease in the good-pleasure of thy Beloved!“

98

Through the Spirit She called out unto Me and said: ”Be patient, for Thou hast known through all whereby Thou hast sought proof.

99

Cast aside whatsoever Thou hast known and whereunto Thou hast cleaved, for in My sight polytheism is even as oneness!

100

The most glorious splendour of Sinai is in My sight as mere chaff, and the brightest radiance of light is in My sight as darkness.

101

The verses of Thy description are true, yet only for novices; the traces of Thy praise are faithful, yet only for My subjects.

102

For verily, from everlasting I have abided in holiness, and beyond all limit I have dwelt in transcendent sanctity.

103

How many a just one hath been in My sight a wrongdoer! How many a learned one hath been in My sight among the ignorant!

104

How many an abiding one hath been in My sight evanescent! How many a mystic knower shall never comprehend a single letter!

105

How many a worshipper hath been in My sight a rebel! How many a prostrating one shall never attain for a single moment unto prostration before Me!

106

The Psalms of heaven are established within the being of My Self, and the Scrolls of splendour have been sent down from My Scroll.

107

From Mine atom, the encompassing sun hath been rolled into an orb; from My drop, the ocean of existence hath surged in praise.

108

All the melodies that have appeared from the peoples of the world are in My sight but as the hum of an ant or the buzzing of a bee.

109

All minds have been enraptured by the attraction of Mine inmost mystery; all souls have been quickened by the melody of My spirit.

110

All divinities have been endowed with divinity from a trickle of My command, and all lords have been nurtured in lordship from the overflow of My rule.

111

Through Me hath the earth of the Spirit walked at the divine bidding, and the Throne of Sinai hath been My footstool.

112

For My light, the Star of Manifestation hath shone forth; for My spirit, the Sun of Joy hath been revealed in effulgence.

113

Repositories of verses, gleaming flashes of revelation, places where traces fall, dawning-places of holiness,

114

Gems of thought, pure essences of reflection, broidered ornaments of light, manifest tokens of wisdom—

115

From the Káf of My Command hath the decree of each been ordained, and from the grace of Mine inmost mystery hath every wondrous thing appeared!

116

Thou didst turn away from My Face and approach them through Thine own surmise, causing the waters of conjecture to flow in the channel of idle fancy.

117

Thou didst not stand firm in the light of the Unseen in that which Thou didst fashion within Thyself, and thus didst Thou forfeit My handiwork.

118

Hold fast unto the Cord of the Cause in the outward form, and recognize the Face of Light in the inward unseen.

119

Rend asunder, then, the veil of nearness from Thyself without an allusion, and behold the Beauty of Holiness within Thyself without an unveiling!

120

Be still, then, for the pillars of the Throne have trembled; and be patient, for the eyes of the Unseen have wept!

121

A meaning beyond all knowledge have I veiled within Thee, which every luminous mind is powerless to fathom.

122

Delight and find fellowship in the inmost secret of the Mystery of Holiness, and divulge it not, if Thou wouldst be of the trustworthy.

123

Were the veil to be lifted from the face of that which Thou hast witnessed, all existence would assuredly perish in the twinkling of an eye!

124

Even thus hath the Command gone forth from a Throne of Might; even thus hath the Decree been issued from a Mystery of Power.

125

Blessed, then, are they that attain through the beauty of their faithfulness! Blessed are they that enter into a wondrous Law!

126

Blessed, then, are the ardent lovers in the shedding of their blood! Blessed are they that cling to the cord of My loving-kindness!

127

Blessed, then, are the sincere who have hastened from every direction beneath the shadow of My Lordship!“

86`بذاك ‌أبادتني القض` (bi-dháka abádatní’l-qaḍá)Apocopated spelling `القض` in the source text for `القضا` / `القضاء` (al-qaḍá’, ”the divine decree“), shortened for meter.(1) Read as `القضاء` (”the divine decree“, chosen).
90`وعن سرّتي نور النّهار تصفّت` (wa ‘an sirratí / surratí)Unvocalized `سرّتي` can be read as sirratí (”mine inmost heart“, frequent throughout the poem) or surratí (”my joy / gladness“, contrasting directly with `كدرتي` ”my gloom/turbidity“ in 90a).(1) ”mine inmost heart [or: my joy]“ (chosen).
92`من نور آنسه` (min núri ánasahu / unsihi)Allusion to Moses perceiving the fire on Sinai in Qur’án 20:10 / 27:7 / 28:29 (`آنس من جانب الطور نارا`); `آنسه` can be read as a relative clause without alladhí (”from the light that He perceived“) or as a verbal noun unsihi / ánasihi (”from the light of His intimacy“).(1) Give both ”from the light of His intimacy [or: the light He perceived]“ in the literal version (chosen).
105`فكم‌ من‌ ساجد لن‌ يفرّ وقتا بسجدتي` (lan yafurra / yafuza)Printed `لن يفرّ` (lan yafirra, ”shall never flee/escape“); very likely a copyist/print mispointing of `لن يفز` (lan yafuz, ”shall never attain/win“, exactly as in v. 40 `ولن يفز بنور الوصل`).(1) Render as ”shall never attain [lit. flee]“ noting the emendation to `يفز` (chosen); (2) ”shall never flee for a moment through prostration to Me“.
111`موضع وظأتي` (mawḍi‘a vaẓ’atí)Orthographic substitution of `ظ` for `ط` in the source text (`وظأتي` for `وطأتي`, vaṭ’atí, ”My footstep / tread“).(1) Read as `وطأتي` (”My footstep / footstool“, chosen).
117`فيما صنعة في نفسك` (fí-má ṣana‘ta / ṣan‘atin fí nafsika)Printed with tá’ marbúṭah (`صنعة`), either a noun (”in what is an artifice within thyself“) or an orthographic variant for the 2nd-person verb `صنعت` (ṣana‘ta, ”in that which thou didst fashion within thyself“).(1) ”in that which thou didst fashion within thyself“ (chosen); (2) ”in that artifice which is within thyself“.
