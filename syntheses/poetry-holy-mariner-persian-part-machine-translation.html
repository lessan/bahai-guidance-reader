About the text

  • Title and author: Lawḥ-i-Malláḥu’l-Quds (Tablet of the Holy Mariner), Persian part (`لوح ملاح القدس (قسمت فارسى)`), by Bahá’u’lláh.
  • Source and edition: Saved at [source] from `https://oceanoflights.org/bahaullah-st-055-2-fa/` (retrieved 2026-10-17), citing Má’idiy-i-Ásmání, p. 339 (`مائده آسمانى، صفحه ۳۳۹`). The source header notes the copyright statement `OceanOfLights.org همه حقوق محفوظ است`.
  • Form and language: In the source file, the text is presented as an Arabic invocation heading (`﴿ هو العجمّى الفارسى العراقى ﴾`) followed by a single continuous paragraph of rhythmic, rhymed Persian prose (saj‘) closing with Arabic Qur’ánic phrases. For alignment between the literal and readable translations, the text is divided below into 16 numbered sentences (1 for the invocation and 15 for the prose body).
  • Content of the text: The first half narrates an allegory of the dwellers of the divine Ark (`اهل فُلك الهى`) who embark by leave of the Holy Mariner (`ملاّح قدسى`) in the ancient vessel (`سفينه قدمى`) upon the sea of Names (`بحر اسما`). When the Name ”the Still“ (`اسم ساكن`) prevails over the Name ”the Mover“ (`اسم مجرى`), the Ark of the spirit comes to a halt until a divine decree commands the Mariner of Eternity (`ملاّح بقا`) to teach them a letter of the Most Hidden Word (`كلمه اخفى`). Enabled thereby to soar on the wings of the spirit beyond the steeps of self and passion, they alight in the Spot of peace and security, the ultimate homeland of the lovers (`منتهى وطن عاشقان`), where the immortal Youth and holy Cup-Bearer (`غلمان باقى و ساقى قدسى`) proffer ruby wine and where they dwell in bliss for ages. When the winds of divine testing (`نسايم امتحان سبحانى و ارياح افتتان سلطانى`) blow from the Sheba of the everlasting Cause (`سباى امر لا يزالى`), they become preoccupied with the beauty of the cup-bearer and heedless of the Immortal Countenance (`وجه باقى`), mistaking shadow for sun and phantoms for light, and seek to scale the ascents of the Most Great Name (`معارج اسم اعظم`). As they ascend, the divine Assay-Masters (`صرّافان الهى`) come down upon them, find no fragrance of the Spiritual Youth (`غلام معنوى`) upon them, and debar them all, fulfilling what was written in the Preserved Tablet (`لوح محفوظ`). The second half turns to direct exhortation, warning the friends of God not to barter nearness to the Beauty of the Friend for the two worlds, nor to defile the lip with foul water or busy the heart with perishable things, and urging them—even if they do not attain the loftiest heights of detachment, unity, and self-sacrifice—at the very least to make their outward deeds and inward secrets one (`ظاهر و باطن خودرا متّحد نمايند`) and to deal with their own souls in justice and equity (`عدل و انصاف`).
  • Historical context: The opening invocation names `’Iráq` (`العراقى`), and the companion Arabic part has an authorised translation by Shoghi Effendi ([source], lines 2485–2723); the exact date, place of writing, and specific historical individuals alluded to are not established in the source file’s header.

Literal translation

1.

﴿ هو العجمّى الفارسى العراقى ﴾

He is the non-Arab (‘Ajamí), the Persian (Fársí), the ‘Iráqí!

2.

چون اهل فُلك الهى باذن ملاّح قدسى در سفينه قدمى باسمى از اسما تشبّث نموده بر بحر اسما رو ان گشتند و قطع مراحل تحديد نمودند كه شايد بيمن همّت سلطان تفريد بشاطى توحيد درآيند و از جام تجريد بنو شند

When the dwellers of the divine Ark, by the permission of the Holy Mariner, in the ancient vessel, having clung unto a Name among the Names, set forth upon the sea of Names and traversed the stages of limitation, that haply through the auspicious grace of the high resolve of the Sovereign of Singleness they might enter upon the shore of divine unity and drink from the cup of detachment;

3.

بارى باعانت ربّانى آن فُلك صمدانى بر آب حكمت روحانى حركت مينمود و سير ميفرمود تا بمقامى رسيدند كه اسم ساكن از اسم مجرى سبقت گرفت و غالب شد لذا سفينهء روح سكون يافت و از حركت ممنوع گشت

In brief, through Lordly assistance that Eternal Ark moved and journeyed upon the water of spiritual wisdom until they reached a station wherein the Name ”the Still“ outstripped the Name ”the Mover“ and prevailed; wherefore the vessel of the spirit found stillness and was withheld from movement.

4.

در اينوقت حكم محكم ربّانى از سماء قدس لا يزالى نازل شد و ملاّح بقا مأمور گشت كه حرفى از كلمه اخفى بر اهل فلك تعليم فرمايد تا باعانت غيبى از وادى حيرت نفسانى بگذرند و بفضاى با فضاى و حدت روحانى درآيند و بقاف بقاى جان و لقاى حضرت جانان و اصل شوند

At this time the firm Lordly decree descended from the heaven of everlasting holiness, and the Mariner of Eternity was commanded that he should teach a letter from the Most Hidden Word unto the dwellers of the Ark, that through unseen assistance they might pass beyond the valley of selfish bewilderment, and enter into the spacious expanse of spiritual unity, and attain unto the Mount Qáf of the immortality of the soul and the presence of His Holiness the Best-Beloved.

5.

و چون اهل كشتى بكلمه دوست معنوى فائز گشتند فى الفور پر معنى گشودند و در هواى قدسى پرو از نمودند و بفضل الهى و رحمت سبحانى از عقبات نفس و هوى و دركات غفلت و عمى گذشتند

And when the dwellers of the ship attained unto the Word of the Spiritual Friend, forthwith they spread the wings of inner meaning and soared in the holy atmosphere, and through divine grace and the mercy of the All-Praised passed beyond the steep defiles of self and passion and the lowest depths of heedlessness and blindness.

6.

و در اين وقت نسايم رضوان از مكمن رحمان بر هياكلشان و زيد و بعد از طيران در هواى قرب الهى و سير مقامات معنو ى در محل امن و امان و منتهى وطن عاشقان نزول نمودند و سكّان آنمقام بخدمت و احسان قيام نمودند

And at this time the breezes of Riḍván blew from the hidden retreat of the All-Merciful upon their temples, and after flying in the atmosphere of divine nearness and traversing the spiritual stations, they alighted in the place of peace and security and the ultimate homeland of the lovers, and the inhabitants of that station rose up in service and kindliness.

7.

در اين وقت غلمان باقى و ساقى قدسى خمر ياقوتى ابذال فرمود بقسمى سكر خمر معارف الهيه و كأس حكمت صمدانيّه جذب و و له آورد و از هستى خود و موجودات رستند و بجمال دوست دل بستند و قرنها و عهدها در آنمقام خوش روحانى و گلزار قدس رحمانى با كمال فرح و انبساط مسكن و مقر داشتند

At this time the immortal Youth and the holy Cup-Bearer bountifully proffered ruby wine, in such wise [that] the intoxication of the wine of divine connaissances and the chalice of eternal wisdom brought rapture and ecstasy, and they were delivered from their own existence and from created things, and bound their hearts unto the Beauty of the Friend, and for ages and cycles they had their dwelling and abode with the utmost joy and gladness in that pleasant spiritual station and holy rose-garden of the All-Merciful.

8.

تا آنكه نسايم امتحان سبحانى و ارياح افتتان سلطانى از سباى امر لا يزالى بو زيد تا آنكه بجمال ساقى اشتغال نموده و از وجه باقى غفلت نمودند بقسميكه ظل را شمس و اشباح را نور انگاشتند و قصد معارج اسم اعظم نموده اند كه در آن هوا طيران نمايند و بان مقعد و محل و ارد شوند

Until the breezes of the testing of the All-Praised and the winds of sovereign trial blew from the Sheba of the everlasting Cause, until they busied themselves with the beauty of the cup-bearer and grew heedless of the Immortal Countenance, in such wise that they accounted the shadow to be the sun and took phantoms for the light, and purposed [to scale] the ascents of the Most Great Name, that they might fly in that atmosphere and enter into that seat and station.

9.

و چون عروج نمودند صرّافان الهى با مجل قدسى بامر مبرم ربانى بر ايشان نازل شدند و چون ارياح غلام معنو ى استشمام ننمودند جميع را منع نمودند و بعد و اقع شد آنچه در لوح محفوظ مسطور گشت

And when they ascended, the divine Assay-Masters descended upon them with the holy touchstone [printed: majall-i-qudsí] by the irrevocable Lordly command, and since they did not inhale the fragrances of the Spiritual Youth, they debarred them all; and thereafter came to pass that which was inscribed in the Preserved Tablet.

10.

پس اى اكنان بساط حُب الهى و اى شاربان خمر رحمت صمدانى قرب جمال دوست را بدوجهان تبديل ننمائيد و از لقاى او بلقاى ساقى نپردازيد و از خمر علم و حكمت او بخمر جهل و غفلت دل مبنديد

Wherefore, O dwellers upon the carpet of divine love, and O drinkers of the wine of eternal mercy! Exchange not nearness to the Beauty of the Friend for the two worlds, and turn not from His presence unto the presence of the cup-bearer, and bind not your hearts, turning from the wine of His knowledge and wisdom, unto the wine of ignorance and heedlessness.

11.

لب محل ذكر محبوبست اورا باب كثيف ميالائيد و دل منزل اسرار باقيست اورا بتوجه اشياء فانى مشغول نداريد آب حيات از كوثر جمال سبحان جو ئيد نه از مظاهر شيطان

The lip is the seat of the remembrance of the Beloved: defile it not with foul water! And the heart is the dwelling-place of everlasting mysteries: busy it not with regard for perishable things! Seek the Water of Life from the Kawthar of the Beauty of the All-Praised, not from the manifestations of Satan.

12.

بارى اين غلام فانى در منتهى مقام حُب دوستان الهى را ببدايع نصح احديه وجواهر حكمت سلطان صمديه متذكر مينمايد كه شايد نفسى قدمروى و مردانگى علم نمايد و از قميص غفلت و شهوت بيرون آيد و چون جمال منير دوست پاك و منير و مقدس در ارض حُب و انقطاع و ود و ارتفاع سير نمايد

In brief, this evanescent Youth, in the utmost station of love, remindeth the friends of God through the wondrous counsels of Oneness and the gems of wisdom of the Eternal Sovereign, that haply a soul may raise the banner of forward-stepping and manliness, and come forth from the shirt of heedlessness and lust, and, like unto the luminous Beauty of the Friend, journey pure and luminous and sanctified in the land of love and detachment and affection and exaltation.

13.

اقلاً اينقدر از انوار صبح مبين و ظهور يوم مبين اخذ نمايند كه ظاهر و باطن خودرا متّحد نمايند

Let them at least acquire this much from the lights of the Manifest Morn and the revelation of the Manifest Day: that they unite their outward and inward selves.

14.

از علّو تجريد و سموّ توحيد و تنزيه كبرى و تقديس عظمى گذشتيم حال سعى بليغ و اهتمام منيع نمائيد كه اسرار باطن مخالف اعمال ظاهر و افعال ظاهر معارض اسرار باطن نباشد و از انفاق جان در سبيل جانان گذشتيم بانفاق عدل و انصاف بر نفوس خود قيام نمايند

We have passed over the heights of detachment and the summits of divine unity, the greatest stainlessness and the most great sanctification; now make ye strenuous effort and lofty endeavour that the secrets of the inward being may not be contrary to outward deeds, and that outward actions may not oppose the secrets of the inward being. And We have passed over the bestowal of life in the path of the Best-Beloved; let them arise to bestow justice and equity upon their own souls.

15.

آخر يعنى قميص حرص و آمال نفسانى از ثوب تقديس رحمانى ترجيح ميدهيد و نغمهء عندليب بقارا بصوت منكر فنا از اهل بغى و بغضا مبادله ميكنيد فبِئس ما انتُم تستبدلون انّا للّه و انّا اليه راجعون

What! Do ye then prefer the shirt of greed and selfish desires above the robe of the sanctification of the All-Merciful, and exchange the melody of the Nightingale of Eternity for the repugnant voice of perdition from the people of rebellion and malice? Evil indeed is that for which ye make exchange! Verily, we are God’s, and unto Him do we return.

16.

انشاء الله اميدواريم كه هياكل عزّ باقى بطراز قدسى و شئون الهى چون شمس صمدى روشن و لطيف و پاك و طاهر ظاهر شود ليس هذا على الله بعزيز.

If God will, We hope that the temples of everlasting glory, decked with holy ornament and divine attributes, may appear like unto the eternal sun, radiant and subtle, stainless and pure. And this is not hard for God.

Yet when they ascended, the divine Assay-Masters came down upon them with the holy touchstone by the irrevocable command of the Lord; and finding upon them no fragrance of the Spiritual Youth, they debarred them one and all, whereafter there came to pass that which was inscribed in the Preserved Tablet.
  1. Wherefore, O ye that dwell upon the carpet of the love of God, and O ye that drink of the wine of eternal mercy! Barter not nearness to the Beauty of the Friend for the two worlds; turn not away from His presence unto the presence of the cup-bearer; nor set your hearts, forsaking the wine of His knowledge and wisdom, upon the wine of ignorance and heedlessness.
  1. The lip is the seat of the remembrance of the Beloved—defile it not with foul water! The heart is the dwelling-place of everlasting mysteries—busy it not with regard for perishable things! Seek ye the Water of Life from the Kawthar of the Beauty of the All-Praised, not from the manifestations of Satan.
  1. In brief, from the utmost station of love, this evanescent Youth remindeth the friends of God through the wondrous counsels of Oneness and the gems of wisdom of the Eternal Sovereign, that haply some soul may raise high the standard of valour and resolve, cast off the garment of heedlessness and lust, and, even as the luminous Beauty of the Friend, walk pure, radiant, and sanctified in the land of love and detachment, of affection and exaltation.
  1. Let them at the very least acquire this much from the lights of the Manifest Morn and the revelation of the Manifest Day: that they make their outward and inward selves one.
  1. We have passed over the heights of detachment and the summits of divine unity, the greatest stainlessness and the most great sanctification; strive ye now with strenuous effort and lofty endeavour that the secrets of your hearts may not contradict your outward deeds, nor your outward actions oppose the secrets of your hearts. And We have passed over the sacrifice of life in the path of the Best-Beloved; let them arise and bestow justice and equity upon their own souls.
  1. Would ye then prefer the garment of greed and selfish desires above the robe of the sanctification of the All-Merciful, and exchange the melody of the Nightingale of Eternity for the harsh voice of perdition raised by the people of rebellion and malice? Evil indeed is that for which ye exchange it! Verily, we are God’s, and unto Him do we return.
  1. Please God, We cherish the hope that the temples of everlasting glory, decked with holy ornament and divine attributes, may appear even as the eternal sun, radiant and refined, stainless and pure. And this is not hard for God.

1`﴿ هو العجمّى الفارسى العراقى ﴾`In the source file, the shadda is placed over the mím (`العجمّى`) rather than the final yá’ (`العجمىّ`). The three nisba adjectives (al-‘Ajamí al-Fárisí al-‘Iráqí) mark the shift from the Arabic section of the Tablet to the Persian section revealed in ‘Iráq.Rendered literally as ”He is the non-Arab (‘Ajamí), the Persian (Fársí), the ‘Iráqí!“ in the literal version, and ”He is the Persian, the ‘Iráqí!“ in the readable version to avoid redundancy in English.
2, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 11`رو ان`, `بنو شند`, `و حدت`, `و اصل`, `پرو از`, `و زيد`, `معنو ى`, `و له`, `بو زيد`, `و ارد`, `و اقع`, `جو ئيد`The source file contains numerous internal space splits after non-joining letters (`و`, `ز`).Preserved verbatim in the Persian lines of the literal translation; read as standard single words (`روان`, `بنوشند`, `وحدت`, `واصل`, `پرواز`, `وزيد`, `معنوى`, `وله`, `بوزيد`, `وارد`, `واقع`, `جوئيد`) in translation.
3`اسم ساكن از اسم مجرى سبقت گرفت`Allusion to the divine Names governing the motion and halting of the Ark (cf. Qur’án 11:41, `بِسْمِ اللَّهِ مَجْرَاهَا وَمُرْسَاهَا`, ”In the Name of God be its sailing and its anchoring“, and the Arabic section’s ”Wherein the ark of the Cause remaineth motionless“, `bahai-prayers.md:2539`). Mujrí may be read as active participle (mujrí, ”the Mover / He Who causeth to sail“) or verbal noun (majrá, ”course/sailing“).(1) ”the Name ‘the Still’ outstripped the Name ’the Mover’“ (chosen, pairing two divine Names in parallel); (2) ”the Name of stillness prevailed over the Name of sailing/motion“.
4`بفضاى با فضاى و حدت روحانى`The printed phrase `فضاى با فضاى` repeats `فضا` (”the expanse possessed of spaciousness“ / ”spacious expanse“). In Nasta‘líq manuscripts, `بافضاى` and `جانفزاى` (”soul-refreshing“ / ”spirit-entrancing“, a frequent rhyming epithet with `فضاى`) are graphically very close.(1) ”spacious expanse“ (following the printed text `با فضاى` as an intensive compound); (2) ”soul-refreshing expanse“ (if emended to `جانفزاى`).
7`غلمان باقى و ساقى قدسى خمر ياقوتى ابذال فرمود`Although `غلمان` (ghilmán) is morphologically the Arabic broken plural of `غلام` (ghulám, ”youth/page of Paradise“, cf. Qur’án 52:24), classical and 19th-century Persian frequently uses `غلمان` as a singular noun, which agrees here with the singular verb `ابذال فرمود` and the subsequent singular reference `بجمال ساقى` in sentence 8.(1) ”the immortal Youth and the holy Cup-Bearer bountifully proffered“ (chosen, treating the pair as a hendiadys with singular verb); (2) ”the immortal youths and the holy Cup-Bearer bountifully proffered“.
7`بقسمى سكر خمر معارف الهيه ... جذب و و له آورد`As printed, `بقسمى` lacks the conjunction `كه` (`بقسمى كه`, which appears in sentence 8).Read as `بقسمى [كه]` (”in such wise that...“).
8`از سباى امر لا يزالى`Spelled with sín (`سبا`), referring to Sheba (Sabá, cf. Qur’án 27:22, 34:15, and Seven Valleys §5 `از سباى رحمان`, ”from the Sheba of the All-Merciful“, `call-divine-beloved.md:122`). In Persian manuscripts sín (`سبا`) and ṣád (`صبا`, the east zephyr wind) are occasionally interchanged when paired with breezes (`نسايم`).(1) ”from the Sheba of the everlasting Cause“ (chosen, matching the exact spelling and the precedent in The Call of the Divine Beloved, line 122); (2) ”from the zephyr (ṣabá) of the everlasting Cause“.
9`صرّافان الهى با مجل قدسى`Crucial textual crux: `مجل` (m-j-l) has no obvious standard sense in connection with `صرّافان` (”assay-masters / money-changers“). Paleographically, in Nasta‘líq/Naskh script, `محك` (miḥakk, ”touchstone“, the instrument with which a ṣarráf tests pure gold against counterfeit coin—cf. Kitáb-i-Íqán, `kitab-i-iqan.md:361, 443`) differs from `مجل` only by a single dot under `ح` and the short stroke of final `ك` (`محك` → `مجل`). Other possibilities include `منجل` (minjal, ”sickle“), `سجلّ` (sijill, ”scroll/decree“), or `مجلّ` (majall/mujall, ”majesty“ or ”scroll“).(1) ”with the holy touchstone“ (emending `مجل` to `محك` miḥakk on strong contextual and paleographical grounds, while noting the printed form); (2) ”with the holy scroll“ (`سجلّ` / `مجلّ`); (3) ”with the holy sickle“ (`منجل`).
10`پس اى اكنان بساط حُب الهى`The printed word `اكنان` (aknán) is the Arabic broken plural of `كِنّ` (kinn, ”shelter, covert, veil“), which is unusual as a vocative parallel to `شاربان` (”drinkers“) unless used by metonymy for ”those sheltered upon“ or ”intimates of“. Paleographically, `اكنان` is one tooth/stroke away from `ساكنان` (sákinán, ”dwellers“) or `سكان` (sukkán, ”inhabitants“, used in sentence 6).(1) ”O dwellers upon the carpet of the love of God“ (chosen as the clearest sense whether read as a variant of `ساكنان`/`سكان` or metonymically from `اكنان`); (2) ”O ye sheltered upon the expanse of divine love“.
12`قدمروى و مردانگى علم نمايد`The compound `قدمروى` (qadam-raví, lit. ”stepping forward / pacing / steadfastness in treading the path“) paired with `مردانگى` (”manliness / valour“) as object of `علم نمايد` (”raise as a banner / make manifest“). Could also be read as `قدمِ روى و مردانگى` or a scribal variant of `پيشروى` (”stepping forth“).(1) ”raise the banner of forward-stepping and manliness“ / ”raise high the standard of valour and resolve“ (chosen); (2) ”plant the foot of sincerity and manliness“.
